Why It Matters

Modern bio-research runs on electricity as much as reagents

Sequencing, bioinformatics pipelines, AI/ML model training, cloud collaboration, high-performance computing and digital microscopy all consume computing resources, storage, network capacity and — ultimately — energy. Most sustainability programs never look here.

Compute-heavy pipelines

Genome assembly, alignment and molecular simulation can run for hours or days on shared clusters.

AI/ML drug discovery

Training and fine-tuning models for target discovery is one of the most energy-intensive workloads in a lab.

Ever-growing datasets

Raw sequencing files, image stacks and derived data accumulate in cloud and local storage indefinitely.

Distributed collaboration

Multi-site research teams transfer large datasets repeatedly across networks and institutions.
